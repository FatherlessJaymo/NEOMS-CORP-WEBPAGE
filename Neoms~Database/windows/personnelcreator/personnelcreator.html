Personnel Creator
Submit a new staff member to the NeoMS archive — follow the steps, watch the file build itself.

Step 1 — Who are they?

Name the staff member and give them a personnel number. Fields marked * are required.

Titles go here too — Dr., Agent, Cpt.

Leave blank for a single-name codename.

Becomes their S-number, e.g. 1010 → S-1010.

Step 2 — ID photo optional

Pick a picture from your device to see it in the preview straight away. To include it in your submission, also paste a link to it — upload it to Imgur (or similar), then copy the image address. Pictures picked from your device aren't saved, only the link is.

No photo
ID photo

Step 3 — Assignment

What's their job, which department are they in, and how much of the archive can they read?

Positions for this department, from the Schemes tab.

Set from the position. CL-5 is reserved for command.

Step 4 — Profile optional

A short service record: how they joined, what they're known for, any incidents on file.

Redact digits with █ for that classified look.

Step 5 — Review & submit

Check the preview, fix anything flagged below, then send it in. Submissions are reviewed before they're added to the archive.
